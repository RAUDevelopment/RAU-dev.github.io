Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The short version

Your images are processed on your own device. They are never uploaded to any server. We do not require an account, do not sell data, and do not track you across the web.

What we do not collect

  • Your images — they never leave your browser. All OCR runs locally using JavaScript.
  • Your extracted text — never sent anywhere.
  • Account data — there are no accounts.
  • Uploads, filenames or file contents — no file is transmitted.

What is collected

Two things, and only one of them is ours:

  • Third-party CDN requests. When the page loads, your browser fetches Tesseract (the OCR engine) from jsdelivr.net, fonts from Google Fonts, and trained language data from tessdata.projectnaptha.com. Those services receive your IP address, which is how any CDN works. They do not receive your images or your text.
  • Ads. The site is supported by advertising. Ad networks may set cookies and collect data such as your IP, approximate location, browser type, and pages you visit to show relevant ads. This is standard for ad-supported sites. You can opt out via your browser settings or an ad blocker.

Cookies

We do not set cookies ourselves. Ad partners and CDNs may set their own. You can block them in your browser without breaking the OCR tool — only the ads will stop working.

Your rights

Because we do not store personal data about you, there is nothing to request, correct, or delete on our side. For data held by ad partners or CDNs, contact those services directly. If you are in the EU, UK or California, the same applies: we do not hold personal data on you.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, so there is nothing extra to say here.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. The core promise — images stay on your device — will not change.
